# Career

Technical executive and entrepreneur with experience founding companies, leading engineering organizations, and architecting systems that scale to millions of users. I've served as CTO, VP of Engineering, and engineering lead across financial technology, government infrastructure, and digital identity.

## Current Engagement

At [SpruceID](https://spruceid.com), I lead engineering for digital identity systems deployed by state governments. My work delivers standards-compliant mobile credentials serving millions of users, with implementations receiving TSA approval for airport authentication. I architect cross-platform Rust SDKs and contribute to open standards including ISO/IEC 18013-5 mobile driver's licenses and OpenID credential protocols.

## Distributed Systems

My technical work focuses on high-performance, fault-tolerant systems:

**Financial Infrastructure**: Architected low-latency systems for CBDCs and payment networks, specializing in Rust-based microservices for concurrent transaction processing. Designed adapters to legacy bank APIs, cross-border protocols, and real-time settlement systems.

**Identity Systems**: Built government-scale credential systems with strict uptime requirements, implementing horizontal scaling, offline-first operation, peer-to-peer verification, and secure credential exchange.

**Protocol Development**: Created RPC frameworks over HTTP/3, implemented consensus mechanisms, and contributed to standards bodies defining next-generation identity and payment protocols.
